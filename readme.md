# ALD Motorshop Online Store

## Proposed System Title

**Web-Based Motorcycle Parts Ordering and Delivery Request Management System for ALD Motorshop**

## System Overview

The ALD Motorshop Online Store is a web-based platform where customers can view motorcycle parts, submit order requests, and choose between store pickup or Lalamove delivery.

The system will also serve as the official website of ALD Motorshop. It will display organized business information, available motorcycle parts, branch details, contact information, promotions, and other customer-related content.

## Business Information

**Business Name:** ALD Motorshop
**Years of Operation:** 4 years
**Number of Employees:** 13 employees in the Main branch
**Main Products:** Genuine Honda, Yamaha, and Suzuki motorcycle parts

## Current Situation

ALD Motorshop currently promotes its motorcycle parts through Facebook and communicates with customers through Messenger.

Customer product inquiries, orders, payment details, and delivery arrangements may be handled through separate Messenger conversations. This can make customer orders more difficult to organize and monitor.

The business also does not have an official website where customers can easily view organized business information and motorcycle parts.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed system will provide ALD Motorshop with its own online store.

Customers will be able to:

* View business information
* Browse motorcycle parts
* Search products
* Submit order requests
* Choose store pickup or Lalamove delivery
* Receive an order reference number
* Track the status of their order

Staff and administrators will be able to process orders, verify payments, record delivery details, update order statuses, manage products, and generate reports.

## Main Goal

The main goal of the system is to provide ALD Motorshop with an official and organized online platform for receiving and monitoring motorcycle-parts orders.

The system aims to reduce reliance on separate Messenger conversations and make it easier for customers and staff to manage order information.

The administrator can:

* Access all staff functions
* Manage motorcycle parts

* Manage staff accounts
* Manage website content
* View all orders and reports
* View activity logs
* Configure system settings

## Main System Features

### Business Landing Page

The landing page will display:

* Business introduction
* Featured motorcycle parts
* Promotions
* Branch locations
* Operating hours
* Contact information
* Facebook and Messenger links

### Motorcycle Parts Catalog

Customers can browse motorcycle parts based on:

* Honda
* Yamaha
* Suzuki
* Product category
* Motorcycle model
* Product name

Each product may include:

* Product image
* Product name
* Brand
* Description
* Price
* Compatible motorcycle model
* Availability status

### Guest Ordering System

Customers will not need to register or log in.

During checkout, the customer will provide:

* Full name
* Contact number
* Complete address
* Selected products
* Quantity
* Pickup or delivery option
* Order notes

### Shopping Cart

Customers can:

* Add products
* Remove products
* Change quantity
* View estimated total
* Continue to checkout

### Pickup or Delivery Request

The customer can choose:

**Store Pickup**

The customer will collect the order from the selected ALD Motorshop branch.

**Lalamove Delivery**

The customer will provide the delivery address and contact information. Staff will arrange the delivery using Lalamove.

### Payment Monitoring

The staff can record:

* Payment method
* Payment amount
* Payment status
* Payment reference
* Proof of payment

Possible payment statuses:

* Unpaid
* Waiting for Verification
* Paid
* Failed
* Refunded

### Order Tracking

The customer will receive an order reference number after submitting an order.

Possible order statuses:

* Pending
* Under Review
* Confirmed
* Waiting for Payment
* Preparing Order
* Ready for Pickup
* Booked for Delivery
* Picked Up by Rider
* Completed
* Rejected
* Cancelled

### Delivery Request Management

Staff can record:

* Delivery address
* Lalamove booking number
* Rider name
* Rider contact number
* Delivery fee
* Tracking link
* Booking date and time
* Delivery status

### Reports

The system may generate:

* Daily orders
* Monthly orders
* Completed orders
* Cancelled orders
* Pickup requests
* Delivery requests
* Payment-status report
* Frequently ordered products

## Main System Flow

Customer visits the website
→ Views ALD Motorshop information
→ Browses motorcycle parts
→ Selects a product
→ Adds the product to the cart
→ Reviews the order
→ Enters customer information
→ Selects store pickup or Lalamove delivery
→ Submits the order request
→ Receives an order reference number
→ Staff reviews the order
→ Staff confirms product availability
→ Staff confirms or rejects the order
→ Customer completes the agreed payment
→ Staff verifies the payment
→ Staff prepares the order
→ Staff arranges pickup or Lalamove delivery
→ Staff updates the order status
→ Customer tracks the order
→ Order is marked as completed
