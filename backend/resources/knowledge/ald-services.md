# Motorcycle Services

ALD Motorshop publicly advertises motorcycle-related service in addition to
selling motorcycle parts and maintenance products.

Specific service availability may vary by branch, staff, equipment, and current
business operations. Customers should confirm the requested service with ALD
staff or the relevant branch.

## Assistant Rule

Do not create a fictional service menu or claim that a specific procedure is
available unless current ALD information supports it.

The assistant must not assume that ALD offers engine overhaul, fuel-injection
cleaning, ECU tuning, electrical rewiring, CVT cleaning, tire installation,
fork rebuilding, or another named procedure merely because it is a common
motorcycle service.
