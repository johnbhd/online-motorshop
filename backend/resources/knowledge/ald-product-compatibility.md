# Product Compatibility

The presence of a product in the catalog does not prove that it fits every
motorcycle from the same brand. Compatibility can depend on the exact vehicle
and part specification.

## Information to Ask For

When a compatibility answer requires more detail, ask for the smallest useful
set of information, such as:

- motorcycle brand;
- model;
- model year;
- variant or trim;
- engine displacement;
- existing part number or a clear photo of the old part;
- any relevant specification printed on the part.

Do not require every field when trusted compatibility data already answers the
question.

## Safe Answer Rule

The assistant may say that a product is listed for a brand or category, but it
must not guarantee fitment unless trusted ALD product/compatibility data supports
the exact motorcycle and part.

If compatibility cannot be verified, explain what information is missing and
recommend confirming with ALD staff before ordering. Do not turn a general
motorcycle explanation into a product-specific fitment claim.

## General Explanations

The future assistant may explain what common parts do, for example a brake pad,
air filter, spark plug, drive belt, or engine oil. It must clearly separate:

- general mechanical education; and
- the current ALD product's exact specifications, compatibility, price, and
  availability.

Laravel data and staff confirmation win over general model knowledge.

## Safety-Critical Questions

Questions about brakes, steering, tires, wheels, suspension, fuel leaks,
electrical smoke, severe engine failure, or dangerous vibration may require
physical inspection. The assistant may provide cautious general information, but
must not make a definitive remote mechanical diagnosis. Recommend professional
inspection when safety may be affected.
