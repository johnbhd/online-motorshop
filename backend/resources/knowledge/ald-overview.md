# ALD Motorshop Overview

ALD Motorshop is a Philippine motorcycle parts and service business. It serves
riders who need motorcycle parts, maintenance products, product questions,
Order Requests, Store Pickup, Lalamove Delivery requests, and related support.

## Business Identity

The available project and public business context supports the following
description:

- ALD Motorshop sells motorcycle parts and related maintenance products.
- ALD also provides motorcycle-related service, but the exact service menu is
  not fixed in this knowledge pack.
- The system currently represents Honda, Yamaha, and Suzuki products.
- Public messaging has emphasized genuine Yamaha and Honda parts.
- Public context refers to Manila, Makati, and Imus, Cavite branches.

This is deliberately cautious business language. The available information does
not verify a founding year, owner, founder, company registration, supplier
contract, award, employee count, or official manufacturer-dealership status.

## Customer Need

ALD customers need a trustworthy way to find motorcycle parts, ask whether a
part may fit, submit a request for staff review, choose pickup or delivery, and
receive support without treating an unverified catalog answer as a promise.

## Business Type and Primary Products

ALD is a motorcycle parts and service business. Its primary catalog scope is
motorcycle parts, maintenance products, lubricants, and related accessories for
the supported brands and compatible applications represented in current data.

## General Services

ALD publicly advertises motorcycle-related service alongside parts. The specific
service menu, branch capability, and appointment/availability rules require
current ALD confirmation.

## What the Digital System Supports

The Laravel-backed system supports or is designed around:

- public product, category, and branch browsing;
- cart-based Order Request submission;
- guest and registered-customer ordering;
- Store Pickup selection;
- Lalamove Delivery requests that are arranged manually by ALD staff;
- payment-method selection and later payment verification;
- public order tracking with a reference and contact verification;
- authenticated customer order history and profile features;
- customer, Staff, and Admin support conversations;
- operational Staff and Admin workflows.

Submitting an Order Request does not by itself reserve stock, guarantee
compatibility, charge a payment, book a rider, or complete a sale. ALD staff
reviews the request and confirms the operational details.

## Supported Brands and Genuine-Part Language

Honda, Yamaha, and Suzuki are the supported motorcycle brands represented in
the current system. The catalog also contains a `Universal` brand taxonomy
value for products that are not tied to one of those manufacturer names.

Public messaging has promoted genuine Yamaha and Honda parts. That does not
prove that every product is genuine or OEM. A specific genuine/OEM claim must
come from trusted current product data or ALD staff.

The system is not authorized by this document to claim that ALD is an official
Honda, Yamaha, or Suzuki dealership.

## Live-Data Boundary

This overview is stable context. Laravel/PostgreSQL must be consulted for
current catalog records, price, availability, branch details, order status,
payment status, delivery information, and customer-specific answers.
